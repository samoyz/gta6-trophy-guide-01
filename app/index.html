Roadmap to the platinum

Five stages, in order. Story first, then the character strands, then activities, then combat and heists, then collectibles and cleanup. The platinum pops when the last of the other 54 does.

  1. Stage 1 — Story. Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission (trophies 2–14). Do the missable trophies before the final mission, because the story ends there.
  2. Stage 2 — Character strands. Finish the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and 8 stranger mission strands across Leonida (trophies 22–27).
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Races, the shooting range, fishing, the arcade, meditation, hunting and the challenge list (trophies 15–21, 28–29, 32).
  4. Stage 4 — Combat and heists. Wanted escapes, shop and bank robberies, armored trucks, Combat Focus and gang operations (trophies 33–41, 43, 45).
  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles and cleanup. Cards, WAiNK spots, vehicles, exploration, money and garages (trophies 30–31, 35, 42, 52–55), then 100% completion and the platinum.
